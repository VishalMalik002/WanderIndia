ABOUT GOVARDHAN

A journey around Govardhan Hill.

Govardhan is a significant pilgrimage destination near Mathura and Vrindavan. Govardhan Hill is traditionally associated with stories of Lord Krishna and is visited by pilgrims throughout the year.

Travel information.

🚆 How to Reach

Govardhan can be reached by road from Mathura and other nearby towns in the Braj region.

📅 Best Time

October to March generally offers more comfortable weather for exploring the area.

🥾 Parikrama

If you plan to undertake the pilgrimage route, carry water, wear comfortable footwear and plan according to the weather.

🗺️ Nearby Places

Mathura and Vrindavan can be combined with Govardhan for a wider Braj pilgrimage experience.
