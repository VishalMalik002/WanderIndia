Vrindavan, the city of devotion.

Explore the sacred temples, colourful streets, peaceful ghats and timeless traditions of Vrindavan, one of India's most celebrated spiritual destinations.

DISCOVER VRINDAVAN

A city filled with devotion.

Vrindavan is closely associated with the traditions surrounding Lord Krishna and Radha. The city is known for its many temples, festivals, devotional music and vibrant spiritual atmosphere.

PLAN YOUR VISIT

Travel information.

🚆 How to Reach

Vrindavan is easily accessible by road from Mathura and is connected with nearby cities through the regional transport network.

📅 Best Time

October to March is generally comfortable for exploring the temples and surrounding areas.

🙏 Temple Etiquette

Dress respectfully, follow temple instructions and be mindful of local customs and traditions.

🗺️ Nearby

Mathura, Govardhan and other important Braj destinations can be combined with a Vrindavan trip.
