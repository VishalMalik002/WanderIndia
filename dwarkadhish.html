MATHURA • UTTAR PRADESH

Dwarkadhish Temple.

Discover one of Mathura's historic temples, known for its devotional atmosphere, beautiful traditions and vibrant celebrations.

ABOUT THE TEMPLE

A temple filled with tradition.

Located in the heart of Mathura, Dwarkadhish Temple is dedicated to Lord Krishna in his Dwarkadhish form. The temple is an important part of Mathura's spiritual and cultural heritage.

LOCATION

Mathura, Uttar Pradesh
