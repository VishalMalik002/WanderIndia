Krishna Janmabhoomi.

Explore one of Mathura's most important spiritual landmarks and discover the history, traditions and stories connected with this sacred place.

ABOUT THE PLACE

A place of deep devotion.

Krishna Janmabhoomi is traditionally regarded as the birthplace of Lord Krishna. The site holds immense religious significance for devotees visiting Mathura from across India and around the world.
