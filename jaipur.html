A city built around royal stories.

Jaipur blends grand architecture with lively markets, traditional crafts and a rich history that makes every neighbourhood feel like part of a larger story.

What makes Jaipur special?

🏰 Amber Fort

Explore one of Jaipur's most celebrated forts and its dramatic hilltop setting.

Admire Jaipur's instantly recognisable palace façade and detailed architecture.

Travel information.

✈️ How to Reach

Jaipur is well connected by air, railway and road with major cities across India.

📅 Best Time

October to March generally offers more comfortable weather for sightseeing.

👟 Practical Tip

Wear comfortable shoes, stay hydrated and allow enough time for the larger forts.

🗺️ Nearby

Amber Fort, City Palace, Jantar Mantar, Jal Mahal and Nahargarh Fort.
