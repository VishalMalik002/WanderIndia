Vishram Ghat.

Experience the peaceful banks of the Yamuna, beautiful evening aarti and the timeless spiritual atmosphere of Mathura.

ABOUT VISHram GHAT

Where the Yamuna meets devotion.

Vishram Ghat is one of the most important ghats on the Yamuna River in Mathura. It is known for its spiritual atmosphere, traditional rituals and beautiful riverside setting.

LOCATION

Mathura, Uttar Pradesh

RIVER

Yamuna

KNOWN FOR

Evening Aarti

EXPERIENCE

Ghats & Spirituality

EXPERIENCE

What makes it special?

🌅 Yamuna Views

Enjoy peaceful views of the Yamuna and the historic surroundings of Mathura.

🪔 Evening Aarti

The evening rituals create a beautiful and devotional atmosphere along the ghat.

🚤 River Experience

Visitors can enjoy the riverside atmosphere and, when available, experience the Yamuna by boat.

📸 Photography

The ghat, river and surrounding heritage provide memorable scenes, especially around sunset.

PLAN YOUR VISIT

Travel information.

🚆 How to Reach

Mathura is well connected by railway and road with Delhi, Agra and other major cities.

🌅 Best Experience

Evening is a popular time to visit because of the riverside atmosphere and aarti.

🙏 Respect the Ghat

Keep the surroundings clean, respect local traditions and follow safety instructions near the river.

🗺️ Nearby Places

Krishna Janmabhoomi and Dwarkadhish Temple can also be explored during a Mathura visit.
